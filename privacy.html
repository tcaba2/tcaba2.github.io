Privacy & Cookies

This is a personal academic website hosted on GitHub Pages. It is deliberately kept simple.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub, as the host, may process your IP address and request metadata to serve the pages and protect the service; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Analytics & cookies

This site uses Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-PCVMN7NH2T), loaded through Google Tag Manager, to measure anonymous, aggregated usage: which pages are visited, rough geographic region, browser and device type, and how visitors arrive. This is used only to understand which content is useful. Advertising features are not enabled.

Consent is handled by a consent management platform (consentmanager.net), which shows a cookie banner on your first visit. Analytics and its cookies (for example _ga) are only set if you agree; if you decline, no analytics cookies are stored.

Changing your choice

You can review or withdraw your consent at any time using the small privacy / “cookie settings” control that the consent tool keeps on the page, or via this link: open cookie settings. Clearing this site's cookies in your browser also resets it.

External links

Pages link to third-party sites (arXiv, INSPIRE-HEP, ORCID, GitHub, LinkedIn, journals and collaborations). Those sites have their own privacy policies.
