What I've been up to.

2026

  • 2026 Co-author on the Pierre Auger Collaboration paper "Observation of the Moon and Sun shadows with cosmic rays at an average energy of 7×1017 eV".
  • 2026 Co-author on "Depth of Maximum of Air-Shower Profiles above 1017.7 eV Measured with the Fluorescence Detector of the Pierre Auger Observatory", published in Phys. Rev. D 114, 043016.

2025

  • December 2025 Completed my MASS master's thesis, "Constraining the High-energy Emission of NGC 1068", and started doctoral research with the Pierre Auger Collaboration at the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology.
  • 2025 Served as Editor-in-Chief of the Journal of the International Association of Physics Students.
  • 2025 Presented the poster "Comparison of SDSS and Gaia QSO data" at the 15th Serbian Conference on Spectral Line Shapes in Astrophysics (SCSLSA), Serbia.

2024

  • 2024 Secured funding from the North American Regional Office of Astronomy for Development, the Heising-Simons Foundation and IAPS for two Dominican students' first research internships and an astronomical-data-analysis workshop, as part of the Month of Astronomy in the Dominican Republic.
  • January 2024 Joined the "Astronomy for a better world" panel at the 243rd American Astronomical Society Meeting, United States.
